# BCSD Research — Index

Compiled: 2026-07-27. Revised: 2026-07-27, after a client correction to the ask and the framing
(see "What changed in this revision" below).

This research supports Pack 170 (Macon, Georgia) explaining itself to Bibb County School
District (BCSD): principals, PTA/PTO boards, and the district's Office of Partnerships. Pack 170
is not an outside organization seeking entry. Most of its children already attend BCSD schools,
and many of its adults are already approved BCSD volunteers. The purpose of this research, and of
the public web page it will eventually support, is to explain how Cub Scouting complements the
district's own stated goals. The ask itself is small: a table at family events already on a
school's calendar, with a PTO meeting slot or a flyer as lighter secondary asks. The alignment
case is the product; the ask is a short closing note, not the headline.

## What's here

- **`SYNTHESIS.md`** — the main document. The real ask, stated up front. Audience fears and who is
  actually asking. The Goal 3 opening. Sub-goal by sub-goal alignment to Goal 3 and Goal 1. A
  dedicated section on Alexander II, the pack's home school and worked example. The risk register.
  A section on what any cautious administrator, generically, is protecting against. The practical
  ask. What we must not claim. Open questions.
- **`talking-points.md`** — a one-pager a pack adult can carry into a conversation with a
  principal's office, a PTO board, or another parent: the small ask in plain language, a handful
  of sourced facts, and answers to the objections that will actually come up.
- **`notes/`** — the five researchers' raw source notes this synthesis draws from:
  - `district-profile.md` — BCSD size, demographics, leadership, partnership infrastructure
  - `strategic-plan.md` — the 2023–2028 #Built4Bibb plan, Goals 1–3, Theory of Action, and
    Goal 3's own baselines and targets
  - `alexander-ii.md` — Alexander II Math and Science Magnet School's verified identity,
    partnership and volunteer rules, and the pack's own context as an already-present school
    community
  - `sel.md` — the CASEL framework and the social-emotional-learning evidence base
  - `mtss.md` — Georgia's Multi-Tiered System of Supports, tiers, school connectedness,
    attendance
  - `scouting-and-education.md` — Scouting outcome research, history with schools, current
    official program content, risk history, and family-engagement evidence

## What changed in this revision

The prior version of this research got two things wrong, corrected here:

1. **The ask was too big.** It recommended asking for meeting space and a partner listing as the
   first move. The actual ask is a table at family events already on a school's calendar, with a
   PTO meeting slot or a flyer as lighter secondary options. Meeting-space recommendations have
   been removed throughout.
2. **The framing was backwards.** The prior version treated Pack 170 as an outside organization
   seeking entry. Most of the pack's children already attend BCSD schools, and many of its adults
   are already approved BCSD volunteers. This is an existing part of a school's own parent
   community explaining a program its families already run, not a vendor or nonprofit pitching for
   access. This also shortens several objections (vetting, unfamiliar adults) and strengthens the
   Goal 3 case, since these are exactly the engaged parents the district says it wants more of.

A factual error was also corrected: `notes/alexander-ii.md` and every other document have been
checked to make sure no reference to Cub Scouts earning "merit badges" appears anywhere. Cub
Scouts earn rank adventures and awards; merit badges belong to Scouts BSA, the program for older
youth.

This revision also keeps the whole deliverable usable at any BCSD school, not only Pack 170's home
school. `SYNTHESIS.md` marks which parts of the case are Alexander-II-specific (its magnet
identity, certifications, robotics team) versus district-general and reusable anywhere, and adds a
short note on what changes when approaching a school where the pack has no enrolled families.

## Bottom line

BCSD's own Theory of Action names stakeholder engagement as the causal input that drives
staff effectiveness and student achievement, and the district's own baseline data shows a
62-point gap on its single biggest engagement metric (student "Show Up" commitment, 33%
against a 95% target); pack families are a concrete, countable instance of the exact thing Goal 3
asks the district to build more of — a family that shows up, an adult who is trained and
background-checked, and a standing relationship between families and a school building. The
supporting SEL and MTSS research, plus a candid answer to every risk question an administrator
will raise, exists to back that argument up, not replace it, and the ask that follows from it is
deliberately small: a table, not a room.
