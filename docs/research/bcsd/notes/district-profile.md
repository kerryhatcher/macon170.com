## Academic Performance

### Graduation Rate

- **Class of 2025 Graduation Rate:** 88.97%
- **Trend:** Third consecutive year exceeding Georgia state average
- **Historical Context:** District graduation rate has increased approximately 37% over the past decade, up from 52% in 2012

**Source:** [Bibb County School District's graduation rate tops state average for third year in a row - 41NBC News](https://www.41nbc.com/bibb-county-schools-graduation-rate-2025/); [Bibb County public schools' graduation rate is the highest in a decade - Macon Newsroom](https://macon-newsroom.com/19078/news/local/bibb-county-public-schools-graduation-rate-is-the-highest-in-a-decade/)

### Accreditation

- **Cognia Accreditation:** "Above Average" rating for overall performance

**Source:** [Bibb County School District earns Cognia accreditation with above average score - 41NBC News](https://www.41nbc.com/bibb-county-school-district-cognia-accreditation/)

### Achievement Assessment

- 2024-2025 Georgia Milestones: Math scores improved across all grades (3-8); reading performance remains below state average; district trails state averages in most subject areas

**Source:** [Bibb County School District's recent Milestones scores showing 'positive momentum' - AOL News](https://www.aol.com/bibb-county-schools-recent-milestones-040000014.html)

### Chronic Absenteeism

- **Georgia Statewide 2024-2025:** 19.5% chronic absenteeism rate (down from 21.3% in 2023-2024)
- **BCSD Specific Data:** Not found for 2024-2025; historical data shows district reduced chronic absenteeism from 16% (2019) to 11% (2020), then saw significant increase during 2020-2021 COVID period
- **BCSD Improvement Efforts:** District participates in Breakthrough Results Program to reduce attendance issues

**Note:** Current-year BCSD-specific chronic absenteeism rate not published in accessible primary sources.

**Source:** [Georgia Department of Education - Chronic Absenteeism Press Release](https://gadoe.org/press-releases/chronic-absenteeism-reaches-lowest-levels-since-pre-pandemic-as-state-continues-efforts-to-reduce-absence-rates/); [District Management Group - BCSD Success Story](https://www.dmgroupk12.com/success-stories/bibb-county-school-district-ga-btr)

## District Initiatives & Programs Relevant to Partnerships

### Office of Partnerships, Projects and Engagement

**Primary Contact for Community Partnerships**

- **Phone:** 478-765-8762
- **Email:** partnerships@bcsdk12.net
- **Address:** 484 Mulberry Street, Macon, GA 31201
- **Mission:** Facilitate strategic partnerships aligned with district's three strategic goals; partnerships are "student-centered and strategic"

**How to Become a Partner:**

1. Contact the Office of Partnerships via phone, email, or submit Partnership Interest Form at: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/KPtZnA7srz
2. Organization mission and goals are reviewed for alignment with district strategic plan
3. If approved, organization completes Partnership Agreement documentation
4. Formal onboarding process (orientation, training, background checks where applicable)

**Partnership Tiers & Contributions:**

- **Silver Tier (I):** Up to $999
- **Gold Tier (II):** $1,000–$5,000
- **Platinum Tier (III):** $5,000+

**Examples of Support:** School supplies, volunteer time, hosting events, internships, mentoring, and multi-year funding

**Source:** [Office of Partnerships, Projects and Engagement - BCSD](https://www.bcsdk12.net/about-us/partnerships); [What Is A Partner - BCSD](https://www.bcsdk12.net/about-us/partnerships/whatisapartner); [Becoming a Partner - BCSD](https://www.bcsdk12.net/about-us/partnerships/becoming-a-partner)

## Community Context: Macon-Bibb County

### Economic Indicators

- **Poverty Rate:** 24.73% of population (compared to national average of 12.5%)
- **Family Poverty:** 19.5% of families
- **Median Household Income:** $51,234
- **Average Household Income:** $73,252 (reflects income inequality)
- **Total Households:** 59,921
  - Families: 59.5%
  - Individuals living alone or with non-relatives: 40.5%

**Source:** [Macon-Bibb County, Georgia Population 2026 - World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-cities/georgia/macon-bibb-county); [U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts - Macon-Bibb County](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/maconbibbcountygeorgia,US/PST045222); [Data USA - Macon-Bibb County](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/macon-bibb-county-ga)

**Note:** Specific data on single-parent household percentage not found in accessible primary sources; Census Reporter and American Community Survey detailed tables may contain this data.

### Population & County Size

- **County Population:** 131,815 residents (school district service area)
- **Median Age:** 39 years
- **Gender:** 47.31% male, 52.69% female

**Source:** [Point2Homes - Bibb County School District Demographics](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/GA/Bibb-County/Bibb-County-School-District-Demographics.html)

## Key Takeaways for Cub Scout Pitch

1. **Strong Family Engagement Infrastructure:** District has dedicated Office of Partnerships and Family Engagement office with established pathways for nonprofits and community organizations.

2. **Proven Partnership Model:** Clear partner application process with tiered giving levels; demonstrated success with existing programs (mentoring, 21st CCLC, wraparound services).

3. **High-Need Community:** 47.8% economically disadvantaged students; 24.73% poverty rate in county; strong demand for after-school enrichment and mentoring.

4. **Academic Momentum:** Improving graduation rates and recognition of title schools; district focused on improvement and partnerships aligned with strategic goals.

5. **Elementary School Access:** 21 elementary schools provides multiple entry points for partnership; Alexander II Magnet, Carter, Skyview, and Vineville are top-performing schools receptive to partnerships.

6. **Leadership Stability:** Superintendent Dr. Dan A. Sims with clear board leadership under new President Daryl Morton.

7. **Existing Youth Programs:** Established mentoring (Mentor's Project since 1990) and after-school infrastructure shows receptivity to youth development partnerships.
