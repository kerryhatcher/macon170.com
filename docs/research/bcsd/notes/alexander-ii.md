## The Magnet Program

### Official Theme & Curriculum Focus

The school's official designation is **Alexander II Math and Science Magnet School**. The school's mission statement is: "Utilizing a curriculum that is rich in math, science, and technology, we seek to motivate all students to become confident, self-directed lifelong learners." [Source: https://alexii.bcsdk12.net/]

### Georgia STEM Certification

- **Certification Status:** GA STEM Certified
- **Date:** October 22, 2021
- **Significance:** First magnet school in Macon-Bibb County to receive state STEM certification; also first to earn virtual STEM certification in Georgia. Across Georgia, 75 schools hold this certification as of the certification period.
- **Source:** https://wgxa.tv/news/local/alexander-ii-joins-list-receiving-stem-certification

### Magnet Program Curriculum & Activities

**Core Instructional Model:**

- Math and science specialists conduct weekly labs with all students
- Hands-on, problem-based activities integrated across grade levels
- Annual environmental education trips

**Extracurricular Programs & Partnerships:**

- FIRST LEGO Robotics Team
- Quiz Bowl Team
- Media Center News Team
- Georgia Pollinator Census (partnership with UGA Extension, community partner Talibah Muhammad from UGA Extension Office)
- Located near Mercer University (partnership referenced in school history)

[Sources: https://alexii.bcsdk12.net/our-school; https://wgxa.tv/news/local/school-of-the-week-alexander-ii-magnet-schools-stem-education/]

### Admission Process

Alexander II operates on a **magnet school lottery system**. Application window for the 2026-2027 school year: November 4 through December 2, 2025. The Bibb County School District maintains an Elementary Magnet Wait List for those not selected in the initial lottery.

[Source: https://www.bcsdk12.net/departments/fine-arts-magnet-programs/magnet-school-applications]

**Georgia School of Excellence:**

- 1984, 1993, 2001, 2011

## Outside Organizations, Tabling & Volunteer Rules

### BCSD Partnership Framework

**Terminology:** BCSD distinguishes between "Strategic Partners" and transactional relationships.

**Strategic Partner Definition:**
An organization that meets all three criteria:

- Supports the district's Strategic Plan
- Provides sustained, aligned support for students and staff
- Commits to enhance student learning experiences for academic outcomes

**NOT considered Strategic Partners:**

- Organizations making only one-time donations without ongoing involvement
- Vendors focused solely on transactional relationships
- Groups seeking promotional student access
- Visitors attending without meaningful interaction

[Source: https://www.bcsdk12.net/about-us/partnerships/whatisapartner]

### Becoming a Partner

**For External Organizations:**

1. Contact the **Office of Partnerships, Projects & Engagement**
2. Complete a **Partnership Interest Form**
3. **Brand Ambassador Fee:** $20 to activate support
4. All partnerships must align with district strategic goals and be student-centered

**For Events & Sponsorships:**

- District and school leaders seeking sponsorship support must submit a **District Sponsorship Request Form 25-26** no less than **45 days in advance**

**Contact:**

- Office of Partnerships: (478) 765-8762
- Email: partnerships@bcsdk12.net

[Sources: https://www.bcsdk12.net/about-us/partnerships; https://www.bcsdk12.net/about-us/partnerships/whatisapartner; https://www.bcsdk12.net/about-us/partnerships/district-project-support]

### Volunteer Approval Process

**BCSD Volunteer Requirements:**

- BCSD maintains **Regulation IFCD-R: School Volunteers** (available through Simbli, the district's policy management system)
- Criminal background checks are required for all volunteers
- Training materials and resources are provided by the district

**Context: Pack 170's relationship to this school.** Most children in Pack 170 already attend
Alexander II. Many of the pack's adults are already approved BCSD school volunteers under IFCD-R
for other reasons, such as classroom help or field trips. This is background supplied by the pack
itself. It is not something found in a published BCSD source, so it carries no citation, and it
does not by itself answer the open question below.

**Specific Details on Outside Organizations at Events, the real open question:**
Not found in published sources. The district's public volunteer page references Regulation
IFCD-R but does not state whether an adult who already holds an approved IFCD-R background check
may staff a table for an outside group (here, the pack) at a school-hosted or PTO-run family
event. IFCD-R covers who may be on campus as a volunteer. It does not appear to separately
address what a table, display, or flyer at a family event requires beyond that. This gap is the
actual procedural question, not "is Pack 170 a stranger to this school." Most of the adults asking
are the same adults already cleared to be in the building.

**Who actually decides?** If the target event is a PTO-run open house or family night, the
decision may belong to the PTO, not the principal. This research did not find a document that
states who owns that decision at Alexander II specifically. Flagged as open below.

**Recommendation for Pack 170:**
Ask the Alexander II front office (or the BCSD Volunteer Office, 478-765-8711) directly:

- Whether an already-approved IFCD-R volunteer may staff a table for an outside group at a
  school or PTO-run family event (open house, fall festival, family night), with no additional
  approval.
- If not, what the additional step is (a second form, a one-time notice to the office).
- Whether posting a flyer or getting a few minutes on a PTO meeting agenda needs anything beyond
  what a PTO/PTA member would already need.
  This is a small, cheap question to resolve directly. It does not require going through the Office
  of Partnerships unless the pack later wants a standing partner listing (see the Partnership
  Framework above for that separate, heavier route).

[Sources: https://www.bcsdk12.net/parents/volunteer; https://www.bcsdk12.net/departments/human-resources]
